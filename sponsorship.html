Sponsorship
DevConf is a community-driven, professional software developer focused, one-day conference hosted annually.
DevConf is only possible through the generous support of sponsors and to show our gratitude we have put together a variety of packages and options to help you achieve your goals at DevConf.
Why Sponsor?
DevConf offers a one-of-a-kind platform to access the best software developers in South Africa in a way that will feel authentic. Previous sponsors have reported massive success in landing new clients, growing their brand and hiring new staff.
We have put together a brief document on Why Sponsor DevConf, which covers the DevConf audience demographics, event statistics, success stories from previous sponsors, FAQs and more!
But if you have any other questions - just drop us an email or book a meeting with us, and we can answer them immediately.
Download the Why Sponsor DevConf document
Sponsorship Packages
DevConf sponsorship packages range in price with a package to suit every budget, and with a variety of extras available as individual purchases or as a discounted add-on, there is a wealth of ways for you to stand out at DevConf.

Apply Now
We have made the process for application very simple.
  1. Go to sponsor.devconf.co.za, select your packages, complete your details and click Submit Request.
  2. We will send you a simple form to complete, so we can get all your details correct.
  3. We will draw up the contract and send it to review. If you have any questions or changes, just let us know, and we can work to make it the best possible contract.
  4. Once signed, we will start to send you any templates for art needed and our wonderful Sponsor Handbook with everything you need to know

Custom Packages?
Our pre-built packages are provided to give potential sponsors an easy way to access the most common structures for sponsoring; but DevConf has always embraced requests from sponsors for something unique or to fit in their budgets.
If you do not see something which fits your needs, please contact us and share your ideas or constraints, and we will work hard to make it a reality for you.
info@devconf.co.za
Payment Terms
Our standard contract is payment within 7 days of invoicing; but we totally understand this may not always be viable. Just let us know, and we can change the payment terms, delay payment until budgets come through, or even split the payments up.
Need More Time?
We understand that internal processes may take time, and you do not want to lose out on your ideal package. To solve this we allow anyone to reserve a package! And there is no deposit or cost to do so.
All you need to do is let us know when you submit the request to sponsor, and we will reserve it for you. If you decide to not take it, just let us know!
